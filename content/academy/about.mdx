# About the Academy

The Advizr Academy is free course-based training on putting AI to work in a business. It is a separate property from these docs, and it is where the taught material lives.

<Callout type="info">
  The courses are at **[advizr.ca/academy](https://advizr.ca/academy)**. Certificates are issued on the permanent domain **[advizracademy.com](https://www.advizracademy.com)**, which is where a certificate you earn stays verifiable.
</Callout>

## Three tracks

Each track is a path through the courses for one kind of reader. You do not have to pick one; the tracks exist so you can start in the right place rather than at lesson one of everything.

| Track | Promise | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** | Get a working mental model of AI in business. No hype, no jargon | Smart professionals starting from zero, and teams adopting AI |
| **Operators** | Find the work AI should be doing in your business and get it running | Founders and owner-operators who buy outcomes, not tools |
| **Builders** | Design and ship AI systems reliable enough to charge for | Developers, technical founders, and automation freelancers |

## What is available today

Ten courses are planned across the three tracks. **One is live now:** *Agentic Workflows*, which sits across the Operators and Builders tracks. The rest are marked coming soon on the Academy itself, and the course pages there are the honest source of what you can start today.

The Academy is free. There is no engagement required to take a course, and nothing on it is gated behind being a client.

## How this relates to these docs

They answer different questions, and it is worth knowing which one you want.

- **The Academy teaches.** Structured courses, in an order, with certificates at the end. Start there if you want to learn the material.
- **This Academy section reads as reference.** The written lessons here (Foundations, Prompt Engineering, Workflow Mastery, Leadership, Industry Guides, the [glossary](/docs/academy/glossary) and the [prompt library](/docs/academy/prompt-library)) are for looking something up when you already have a job to do.
- **The [Services education](/docs/services/education) curriculum is different again.** That is the live, taught arc delivered inside a client engagement: sessions with your FDE, coaching calls, and material built around your own business.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you are a client, the engagement curriculum is the one that matters most, because it is taught against your workflows rather than generic examples. The Academy is what you send to the rest of your team.
</Callout>

## Certificates

Completing a course on the Academy issues a certificate. Certificates live on advizracademy.com permanently, on their own domain, so a link you put on a CV or a LinkedIn profile keeps resolving regardless of what happens to any other Advizr property.
