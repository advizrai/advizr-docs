# Your first 30 days

The first month sets the pace for everything after it. The Advizr Platform is configured on day one, your first system is live for your people by day 14, and by day 30 the people you named use the systems every week. We deliver your custom strategy and systems in under a month, or we keep working for free until they are done. This guide covers what happens in each stretch of the month and what your side needs to do to keep that pace.

If you haven't read [How we work](/docs/services/how-it-works) yet, start there. This guide is the client-side companion to that timeline.

## How the month maps to the engagement

| Day | What happens | By then, you have |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Setup | The Platform configured, access live for the people you named, and the first month's scope note agreed |
| By day 5 | The shortlist | A shortlist of candidate builds, with what each takes off your people |
| By day 7 | The baseline | Your baseline measured and signed off, and the next build chosen |
| By day 14 | The first system | Your first system live for your people, on your own data |
| By day 30 | Weekly use | The people you named using the systems every week |

Your statement of work sets your exact dates, counted from its start date. If something we need from you arrives late, such as the names of your people or access to a system, the day-14 date moves by the days it was late.

## Days 1 to 7: setup and the baseline

Your first week has two jobs: give your forward deployed engineer (FDE) the context they need, and measure how the work runs today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the onboarding wizard">
    The [five-step wizard](/docs/platform/onboarding/welcome) takes about 15 minutes and can be paused and resumed. It collects your company details, first objective, key documents, and kickoff booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a specific objective">
    Write an objective with a number and a timeframe, such as "reduce manual data entry in invoicing by 50% within three months", not "use AI more". [Setting objectives](/docs/platform/onboarding/setting-objectives) shows the pattern.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload two or three key documents">
    Start with documents that describe repeatable processes, such as SOPs, process maps and financial summaries. See the [data preparation guide](/docs/resources/guides/data-preparation) for formats and priorities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your people">
    Send the names and email addresses of the people who get access, and confirm who approves work and who reviews the first results. Your FDE needs these on day 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up access live">
    Your FDE sets up access to the systems the first build needs, live, with whoever administers each one. Credentials are never sent by email or chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend your kickoff call">
    A 30 to 45 minute call where your FDE reviews your objectives, asks about your documents, and agrees the plan for the month. Afterward they send a summary and action items. The [kickoff preparation guide](/docs/resources/guides/preparing-for-kickoff) covers how to arrive ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure the baseline">
    Time the real work with your FDE and the people who do it, and bring the questions your people ask every week. By day 7 the baseline is signed off and the next build is chosen from the shortlist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Days 8 to 14: your first system goes live

Your FDE builds the first system on the Platform, against your real data. Your part in this stretch is small, and it decides the pace:

| What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Answer access requests quickly | Every day a request sits unanswered is a day the build stalls |
| Test each system on real work within a day or two | Early feedback is cheap, and feedback three weeks later means rework |
| Approve promptly | Decisions arrive in your [Inbox](/docs/platform/today/inbox) as approvals, and nothing moves until you respond |

By day 14, your first system is live for your people, on their own logins and your own data.

<Callout type="tip">
  Between sessions, point your team at the [Prompt engineering basics](/docs/academy/prompt-engineering/basics) lesson. Thirty minutes of self-serve learning makes the live sessions more productive.
</Callout>

## Days 15 to 30: weekly use on real work

The second half of the month is about use. The weekly session teaches your team to run the systems, correct the Brain and read the results. Between sessions, check [Runs](/docs/platform/engagement/runs) a few times a week. It shows every workflow execution, and [Reading your dashboard](/docs/academy/workflow-mastery/reading-your-dashboard) explains what the numbers mean and when to act. Bring friction, questions and the next work you want taken off your people to every session.

If the people you named are not using the systems every week by day 30, your FDE adds coaching and reworks what is in the way, at no extra charge, until they are.

## What Advizr does and what you do

| What Advizr does | What you do |
|---|---|
| Configures the Platform and sets up access live on day 1 | Name your people and complete the onboarding wizard |
| Shortlists candidate builds by day 5 | Read the shortlist and say what matters most |
| Measures the baseline with your team by day 7 | Time the real work with your FDE and sign off the numbers |
| Puts your first system live by day 14 | Test it on real work within a day or two |
| Runs the weekly session and sends a note the same day | Keep the slot and bring the people being trained |
| Coaches until the systems are in weekly use | Check Runs and raise friction while it is small |

## Milestones to hit by day 30

You're on pace if, by the end of the month:

- Your onboarding wizard is complete and your first objective is on the [Goals page](/docs/platform/engagement/objectives)
- Your baseline is signed off
- Your first system is live and has run against real data
- The people you named come to the weekly session and use the systems every week
- Your next weekly session is in the calendar

If any of these are missing, raise it with your FDE in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact) or at the next session. The earlier a stall is named, the cheaper it is to fix.

## Day-30 checklist

```text
First 30 days: client checklist

Days 1 to 7
[ ] Complete the five-step onboarding wizard
[ ] Set one specific, measurable objective
[ ] Upload 2 or 3 key process documents
[ ] Send the names and emails of the people who get access
[ ] Confirm who approves work and who reviews first results
[ ] Set up system access live with your FDE
[ ] Attend the kickoff call and read the follow-up summary
[ ] Time the real work with your FDE and sign off the baseline

Days 8 to 14
[ ] Answer every access request the same day
[ ] Test the first system on real work within 48 hours
[ ] Respond to approvals in your Inbox

Days 15 to 30
[ ] Keep the weekly session, with the people being trained
[ ] Check the Runs dashboard at least twice a week
[ ] Complete the Academy "Getting started" checklist as a team
[ ] List any friction or new opportunities to raise with your FDE
```
