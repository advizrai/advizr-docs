# General FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about the Advizr platform. If you don't find what you're looking for here, ask your team in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact) or [book a call](/docs/platform/features/booking-and-scheduling) with your FDE.

<Details title="What is Advizr?">
  Advizr is the AI operating layer for enterprises. You get three layers in one monthly retainer. The Advizr Platform, configured for you on day one on infrastructure isolated to your company. Custom AI systems built on top of it. And a forward deployed engineer (FDE) who builds them and trains your team until they use them. The platform at advizrclients.com is where your team works with all of it: setting goals, adding documents to the Brain, asking your AI team for work in Interact, and approving its plans in your Inbox.
</Details>

<Details title="Who is the platform for?">
  The platform is designed for business owners and their teams. You don't need technical skills to use it. The interface is built around plain-language asks, clear dashboards, and guided workflows. Whether you're a sole founder or have a team of 50, the platform adapts to your role and needs.
</Details>

<Details title="How do I get started?">
  After signing up, you'll receive an email invitation. Click the link to set your password, then log in at advizrclients.com. A five-step onboarding wizard guides you through setting your first goals, adding your source documents, booking your kickoff call, and asking your team for something for the first time. The whole process takes about 15 minutes. See [Getting started](/docs/platform/getting-started) for a full walkthrough.
</Details>

<Details title="What can I do in the platform?">
  You can chat with AI that knows your business context, set and track business objectives, upload documents for analysis, review transformation proposals from your Advizr team, access learning content, book calls with your FDE, and monitor automated workflows. The platform is your central hub for everything related to your Advizr engagement.
</Details>

<Details title="What is a forward deployed engineer?">
  Your FDE is the Advizr engineer assigned to your engagement, a real person rather than a bot. They set the strategy with you, build your systems on the Platform, run a weekly session with your team, and answer your messages the same business day. "Forward deployed" means they work alongside your team on your own operations, rather than sending advice from a distance.
</Details>

<Details title="How much does Advizr cost?">
  It depends on what we build, so we price it in the proposal rather than publish it. An engagement is one monthly retainer covering the Advizr Platform, the custom systems built on it, your FDE, and training for your team. There is no build fee and no setup fee. After the mapping calls, the proposal puts a measured value on each system and states the price. See [How pricing works](/docs/services/pricing) for how the price is set.
</Details>

<Details title="Is there a long-term contract?">
  No. There is no minimum term. You can end your engagement in any month by email, with no penalty and no exit fee, and it ends at the close of that month. After that, everything built for you keeps running, at what the hosting and model usage cost us. See [Billing](/docs/platform/account/billing) for details.
</Details>

<Details title="How often should I log in?">
  Most clients check in a few times per week. Dashboard shows what needs you and what has happened, so a quick daily check keeps you up to date. If you prefer less frequent visits, enable [email digests](/docs/platform/features/notifications) to get summaries delivered to your inbox.
</Details>

<Details title="Can my team use the platform?">
  Yes. You can invite team members from [Team management](/docs/platform/your-team/team). Each person gets their own login and a role that controls what they can see and do. Roles include Client Admin (full access), Client Viewer (read-only), and Client Consumer (a simplified, chat-focused experience). See [Roles and permissions](/docs/platform/account/roles-and-permissions) for the full comparison.
</Details>
