# Your Advizr client platform

The platform at advizrclients.com is where your engagement happens. You set goals, put your team to work, approve what it asks about, and read what it delivered. This section covers getting set up, finding your way around, and getting value out of every surface. Your workspace is isolated, so your data stays yours.
