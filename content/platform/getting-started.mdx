# Getting Started

This page walks you through your first login to the Advizr platform, what you will see when you land on your dashboard, and how to navigate the interface. If you have just received your invitation email, start here.

## Accepting your invitation

When your Advizr team sets up your account, you will receive an email with a link to advizrclients.com. Click the link to set your password and activate your account. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder or contact your Advizr team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    Look for an email from Advizr with the subject line "Welcome to Advizr." Click the activation link inside.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Choose a strong password. You will use this along with your email address to log in going forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to your dashboard">
    Visit advizrclients.com and enter your email and password. You will land on your Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the rail">
    The left rail groups the workspace into five sections: Today, Engagement, Your team, Value and Learn. Only Today is expanded when you arrive; open the others as you need them. On mobile, tap the menu icon in the top-left corner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  Bookmark advizrclients.com so you can get back to your dashboard quickly. On mobile, add it to your home screen for one-tap access.
</Callout>

## Navigating the dashboard

The rail is your main navigation. It stays visible on desktop and collapses into a menu on mobile. The top bar carries search, your team's live status, notifications and your account menu.

The five sections group the workspace by what you are trying to do:

**Today** is the daily loop.
- [Dashboard](/docs/platform/today/dashboard) is the front door: what happened, what needs you, and the ask bar.
- [Inbox](/docs/platform/today/inbox) is where your team asks permission or a question.
- [Digest](/docs/platform/today/digest) is the weekly written summary.

**Engagement** is the work itself.
- [Goals](/docs/platform/engagement/objectives) are the targets everything is planned against.
- [Workflows](/docs/platform/engagement/workflows) are the repeatable systems, both the ones Advizr built for you and your own.
- [Runs](/docs/platform/engagement/runs) is the full record of everything your team has done.

**Your team** is who does the work.
- [Tasks](/docs/platform/your-team/tasks) is the shared board for people and AI coworkers.
- [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact) is where you ask for work and watch it happen.
- [Team](/docs/platform/your-team/team) is the roster, and [Hiring](/docs/platform/your-team/hiring) adds to it.

**Value** answers whether it is working.
- [Analytics](/docs/platform/value/analytics) is the live picture.
- [Reports](/docs/platform/value/reports) is the written monthly edition.

**Learn** is what your team knows and where to read more.
- [Brain](/docs/platform/learn/brain) is every source your team has read.
- Documentation is this site.

## Where to go for help

If you get stuck, ask in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact) for the fastest response. For common questions, see [Getting help](/docs/platform/features/getting-help), or [book a call](/docs/platform/features/booking-and-scheduling) with your FDE to talk it through.
