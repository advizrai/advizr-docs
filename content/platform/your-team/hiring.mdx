# Hire a coworker

Hiring adds a new AI coworker to your roster. The wizard is built around an outcome rather than a configuration form, because describing what you want done is something you can do accurately, and specifying how it should be done is not.

<Screenshot
  src="/images/platform/hire.png"
  alt="The hire wizard"
  caption="The hire wizard opens with the outcome, not a settings form."
/>

## The wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the outcome">
    One field, plain language. "Keep our supplier records current and flag anything expiring in the next 60 days."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the interview">
    The wizard asks what it needs to know, one question at a time, based on what you wrote. Skipping a question is allowed. It makes an assumption and tells you what it assumed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the blueprint">
    You get a readable description of the coworker: its remit, what it is allowed to do, and what it will check with you about first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a name and a face">
    Cosmetic, and worth doing anyway. A team of named colleagues is easier to reason about than a list of functions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The coworker appears on your roster immediately and can be given work straight away.

## What you are never asked to choose

There is no model picker. Your team routes each piece of work to the right capability tier itself, which is why a model improvement reaches your workspace without you re-configuring anything.

<Callout type="tip">
  A narrow remit outperforms a broad one. Two coworkers with clear jobs beat one that does everything, and they are far easier to judge when something goes wrong.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

- **The blueprint does not match what you meant.** Go back and change the outcome sentence rather than editing the blueprint. The rest is derived from it.
- **The new coworker is not picking up work.** It needs work assigned, or tagged agent-ready. See [Tasks](/docs/platform/your-team/tasks).
- **You cannot open the wizard.** Hiring needs the right role. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/platform/account/roles-and-permissions).
