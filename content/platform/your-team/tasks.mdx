# Run the workboard in Tasks

Tasks is the shared workboard. People and AI coworkers appear on the same board, holding the same kind of card, which is the point: the work is one queue, not a human queue and a robot queue that someone has to reconcile.

## Views and grouping

The board can be read as a **Board** or a **List**, and grouped by stage, owner, priority or due date. Grouping by owner is the fastest way to see what your AI coworkers are carrying versus what your people are carrying.

## The states worth knowing

- **Agent-ready.** Tagged as work an AI coworker can take. If nothing is agent-ready, your team has nothing to pick up, which is usually why a board looks busy while the workspace looks idle.
- **Waiting on approval.** The card is blocked on a decision in [Inbox](/docs/platform/today/inbox). The board shows this rather than letting the card look stalled for no reason.
- **In progress by a coworker.** Live work, traceable to a run.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tasks from Your team">
    The board opens on the default grouping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group by owner">
    This splits the board into who is actually carrying what.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag work agent-ready">
    Anything well-specified enough for a coworker to take can be tagged. Vague cards are the ones to leave with a person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a card for its history">
    A card opens to its detail, including the runs that have worked on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  A card that keeps bouncing back from a coworker is usually under-specified rather than too hard. Adding the missing constraint to the card fixes it more often than reassigning it to a person.
</Callout>

## Bulk changes

Selecting multiple cards opens a bulk bar for stage, owner and priority. Useful after a planning session, when a batch of cards needs the same move.

## If something goes wrong

- **A card sits agent-ready and nothing picks it up.** Check whether your team is paused, and check [Inbox](/docs/platform/today/inbox) for a blocking approval.
- **A coworker took a card and produced the wrong thing.** Give feedback on the run, not the card. See [Runs](/docs/platform/engagement/runs).
- **Cards you did not create keep appearing.** Workflows create cards when their output needs a human. That is intended.
