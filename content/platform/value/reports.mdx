# Your results, monthly

Reports is the written record of what your engagement produced. A fresh edition lands on the first of each month, compiled and written for you by the analyst on your team rather than assembled from a template.

<Screenshot
  src="/images/platform/reports.png"
  alt="The Reports screen"
  caption="Your results: the current edition, with earlier ones on the shelf below."
/>

## What an edition is

An edition is a document, not a dashboard. It reads as prose, cites the runs behind each claim, and measures against the baseline you signed at the start of the engagement.

It is built to be forwarded. When someone in your business who never logs in asks what the AI team has been doing, an edition answers it.

## Working with it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the current edition">
    The most recent edition is at the top of the page, with its cover and summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull this month early">
    You do not have to wait for the first. The current month can be compiled on demand from whatever has happened so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Editions download for circulation outside the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back through the shelf">
    Earlier editions stay available. Three consecutive editions show a trend that one cannot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  The edition is written by a named analyst on your roster, and you can ask that coworker about anything in it directly in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact). It is a document with an author, not an export.
</Callout>

## Reports, Digest and Analytics

| Surface | Cadence | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| [Digest](/docs/platform/today/digest) | Weekly | What happened, and what needs me |
| [Analytics](/docs/platform/value/analytics) | Live | What has this delivered over a period |
| **Reports** | Monthly | Is this working, written out and citable |

## If something goes wrong

- **No edition yet.** The first edition needs a month of activity, or use the on-demand option for a partial one.
- **An edition claims something you disagree with.** Every claim cites its runs. Open the citation first, then raise it with your FDE if the run does not support the claim.
- **The download is missing sections.** Regenerate the edition. A compile that ran mid-month can be re-run once the month closes.
