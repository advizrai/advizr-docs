# Start your day from Dashboard

Dashboard is the first thing you see when you sign in, and it is the one screen worth opening every morning. It answers three questions in order: what happened while you were away, what needs you now, and what do you want to set in motion today.

## Work the screen top to bottom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the greeting line">
    The heading is a plain sentence about your workspace right now, not a label. It tells you whether your team is working, waiting on you, or idle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear what needs you">
    Anything waiting on a human decision surfaces here first. Approvals, questions your team could not answer alone, and work that stalled. Each one opens straight into the item rather than a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan today's modules">
    Below that, the day's modules summarise recent runs, goal movement, and new results. They are read-only summaries. Click through when something looks worth a closer look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for something">
    The ask bar at the top takes a plain sentence. Type what you want done and your team plans it before doing anything. See [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You should finish Dashboard with nothing unactioned in the first block, and a clear idea of what your team is doing today.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you only open one screen a day, open this one. Everything that needs a human decision reaches Dashboard, so you can leave the rest of the workspace alone until you want detail.
</Callout>

## The ask bar

The ask bar is the fastest path into work. It accepts an outcome rather than an instruction: "find out which accounts went quiet this quarter" works better than naming a tool or a step.

What happens next depends on the size of the request. Small asks run immediately. Anything larger comes back as a plan first, and nothing runs until you approve it. That is deliberate: your team checks with you before it acts, not after.

## What Dashboard does not show

Dashboard is a summary, not an archive. Full history lives elsewhere:

- Every run your team has done is in [Runs](/docs/platform/engagement/runs)
- Every message, approval and decision is in [Inbox](/docs/platform/today/inbox)
- The weekly write-up is in [Digest](/docs/platform/today/digest)

<Details title="Why the greeting changes wording">
  The heading is generated from the actual state of your workspace, so it reads differently when work is running, when something is waiting on you, and when everything is clear. It is a status line written as a sentence. If it says your team is waiting on you, there is something in the block below it.
</Details>

## If something goes wrong

- **Dashboard looks empty.** A new workspace has nothing to summarise yet. It fills in after your kickoff call and your first goals are set.
- **Something you actioned is still showing.** Refresh the page. If it persists, mention it in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact) so your team can look.
- **An item opens a page you cannot act on.** Some actions need the Client Admin role. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/platform/account/roles-and-permissions).
