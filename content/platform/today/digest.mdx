# Read the weekly Digest

Digest is the written summary of your workspace, assembled on a schedule. Where Dashboard answers "what is happening now", Digest answers "what happened, and what does it add up to".

<Screenshot
  src="/images/platform/digest.png"

  caption="A Digest edition: the week's work, condensed into something you can read in a sitting."
/>

## What is in an edition

- **What your team did.** Work completed, grouped by the goal it was serving rather than by the system that ran it.
- **What moved.** Goal progress since the last edition, including goals that did not move.
- **What it learned.** New sources added to the Brain, and answers that changed because of them.
- **What needs you.** Anything still sitting in Inbox at the time the edition was cut.

## Reading it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Digest from the Today section">
    The most recent edition opens by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary first">
    The top of the edition is a plain-language paragraph. If nothing in it surprises you, you are done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow anything that does surprise you">
    Every item links to the underlying run, goal or source, so you can go from a sentence to the evidence in one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check earlier editions">
    Past editions stay available. Reading three in a row is the fastest way to see a trend that no single week shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
  Digest is the artefact worth forwarding. If someone in your business wants to know what the AI team is actually doing, an edition answers it better than a dashboard screenshot.
</Callout>

## Digest and Reports are not the same thing

Digest is operational and weekly: what happened, what needs you. [Reports](/docs/platform/value/reports) is the monthly results edition, compiled and written for you, aimed at the question of whether the engagement is paying for itself.

## If something goes wrong

- **No edition yet.** Editions need a week of activity behind them. A new workspace produces its first once there is something to summarise.
- **An edition looks thin.** That is usually accurate. A quiet week produces a short digest, and that is more useful than padding.
- **A link in an edition 404s.** The underlying item may have been removed. Mention it in [Interact](/docs/platform/your-team/interact).
