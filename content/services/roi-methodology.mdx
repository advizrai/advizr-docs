# How we measure value

We put a number on every system before we build it. The number is per system, in hours and dollars, never a blanket multiple. This page shows how we get it and how we check it after launch.

## The formula

Value compares what a workflow costs today with what it costs once a system runs it:

**Monthly value = (hours saved × loaded hourly cost) + measured revenue impact**

Hours saved is the conservative driver. Loaded hourly cost is salary plus benefits, tools and overhead, at your own rates. Revenue impact, such as more qualified leads contacted or faster follow-up, counts only when it is directly attributable and you agree with the attribution.

## In the proposal

After the mapping calls, the proposal puts this value on each system we would build. For example: the hours a workflow saves each week, and what those hours are worth a year. Each system stands on its own number.

## The baseline, in the first week

Once work starts, we measure the baseline with your team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the workflow">
    The specific process the system takes over: who does it, how often, and in which tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure it on real work">
    Time the work with the people who do it, on real cases, and multiply by their loaded hourly cost. The baseline is measured, not estimated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree on the counters">
    What we count after launch: runs completed, items processed, hours displaced, response times. The counters are built into the system itself, so every run is logged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign it off">
    Each number records who gave it, on what date, and how it was measured. Your team signs off the baseline in the first week, because once the manual work is replaced it can no longer be measured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Measurement after launch

Every system logs its own activity: runs, items processed, outcomes. Each monthly review reports value against the baseline, meaning the hours displaced and what those hours are worth at your own loaded cost. The numbers come from the system logs, and you can see the run history yourself in [Runs](/docs/platform/engagement/runs).

## What we don't count

- Soft benefits, such as morale or the feeling of moving faster. They are real, but they cannot be audited.
- Savings from workflow changes you made without our systems.
- Revenue with unclear attribution.

Individual results depend on your workflows, volumes and labour costs.
