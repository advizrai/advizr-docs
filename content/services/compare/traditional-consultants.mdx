# Advizr vs traditional consultants

The AI consulting industry mostly sells analysis. We sell working systems with the analysis built in. Here's the concrete difference.

## Deliverables

| | Traditional consulting | Advizr |
|---|---|---|
| Primary deliverable | Strategy deck + recommendations | Production systems your business runs on |
| Who implements | You (or a second engagement) | We do, with your strategy and systems delivered in under a month |
| Team capability | Workshop, maybe | A weekly session on your own systems, in every engagement |
| Pricing | Day rates, open-ended scope | One monthly retainer, [set in the proposal](/docs/services/pricing) after the mapping calls |
| Accountability | Effort | A delivery guarantee, and a [measured value on each system](/docs/services/roi-methodology) |
| Documentation | The deck | Living docs, and this site is the public version |

## The structural difference

A consultancy is paid to extend the engagement, and advice is never finished. Our retainer lasts only while the systems keep producing measured value and your team keeps using them. That is why we document our methods in public, put a number on each system before we build it, and guarantee delivery. The incentives point the same direction yours do.

## When a traditional firm fits better

When you need a brand name on the board deck, a traditional firm may be the better choice. We are built for companies that want working systems and a team that uses them, more than a binder.
