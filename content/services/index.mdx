## Why training is part of it

Systems are useless unless people use them. Your people need AI and will not adopt it alone, because they cannot learn it on their own and will not find the time. So the engineers who build your systems stay to train your team, every week, on its own data. As the team learns, it finds more work worth taking off its people, and we build that too.
