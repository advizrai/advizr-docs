# How we work

Every engagement runs on the same method. A forward deployed engineer (FDE), a real person assigned to your business, sets the strategy with you, builds your systems on the Advizr Platform and trains your team to use them. Building and training run together from the first week, because a system your team does not understand stops being used.

## Before you sign

<Steps>
  <Step title="A first call">
    We learn what slows your business down and who does that work today. There is no price on this call. If we are not the right fit, we say so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mapping calls">
    Our engineers map how your operations run, in as many calls as it takes: the workflows, the volumes, the handoffs and the tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The proposal">
    The systems we would build, a measured value on each one, the order we would build them in, and the price. You decide in your own time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The first month

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | The Advizr Platform is configured for you. Access is live for the people you name, and the first month's scope note is agreed. |
| By day 5 | A shortlist of candidate builds, with what each takes off your people. |
| By day 7 | Your baseline is measured with your team and signed off, and the next build is chosen from the shortlist. |
| By day 14 | Your first system is live for your people. |
| By day 30 | The people you named use the systems every week. |

We deliver your custom strategy and systems in under a month, or we keep working for free until they are done. Your statement of work sets your exact dates, and [the guarantee](/docs/services/guarantees) lists what it commits to.

## Every month after

Each month starts with a one-page scope note: what will ship and the test each item must pass. Your FDE runs a weekly session with your team and sends a one-page note the same day, with what moved, what is next and at most three things we need from you. Each month ends with a delivery record and a thirty-minute review of what shipped, who is using it and what comes next. Then we build the next system under the same retainer, in the order your team sets.

## What we need from you

The engagements that move fastest have a decision-maker in the room, someone who can say yes to a workflow change without a committee. The people being trained keep the weekly session. And we work on your real data and tools, because systems built against fake data break against real data. Access is set up live with whoever administers each system, and credentials are never sent by email. The [data handling terms](/docs/platform/faq/data-and-privacy) cover how we treat your data.

## What we don't do

We don't build consumer mobile apps, and we don't run one-off training with no build attached. We don't take on work we can't measure. We won't automate a workflow that's broken on paper. We'll tell you to fix the process first, and help you do it.

## Why this works

Each system becomes a teaching tool, and each session turns up the next system worth building. As your team learns, it finds more work worth taking off its people. As models get better and cheaper, every system on the Platform gets the upgrade. What you keep is a capability that stays when any vendor leaves.
