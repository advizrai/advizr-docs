# Education

Training is part of every Advizr retainer, because systems are useless unless people use them. Your forward deployed engineer trains your team on its own data and its own systems, every week, until working with AI is how the work gets done.

## Why training is in the retainer

Most AI adoption fails on people, not technology. Your people need to use AI and won't, because they can't learn it alone and won't take the time. So we teach on the systems we build for you, with your team in the room. Each session also turns up the next piece of work worth taking off your people, and we build that next.

## The four-phase curriculum

| Phase | What it covers |
|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | What AI is, what it can do, and how to think about it |
| 2. Prompt engineering | How to work with AI tools and get reliable results |
| 3. Workflow mastery | Understanding your systems, reading their results, and finding new opportunities |
| 4. Strategic leadership | Building your AI strategy, leading adoption and managing change |

There are no tiers, so no phase is held back. Your engineer sets the order with you, based on who is in the room and which systems are going in.
