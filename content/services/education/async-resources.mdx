# Async resources

Between live sessions, your team has material built around its own systems and workflows rather than generic AI courses.

## A guide for every system

For each system you accept, your engineer makes a short walkthrough video for your team and a written guide. The guide has one part for the people who use the system and one for whoever looks after it.

## Notes that follow the work

| What | When | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| The weekly note | The day of each weekly session | What moved, what is next, and at most three things we need from you, each with a person and a date |
| The scope note | The start of each month | What will ship that month, and the test each item must pass |
| The delivery record | The end of each month | Each item, the test it was held to, and the evidence |
| The backlog | Added to every month | The ideas your team raised, in the words it raised them, in the order you set |

## Written when the work needs them

Your engineer also writes these when the work calls for them, and names them in that month's scope note:

- Standard operating procedures for your workflows: what each process does, when to use it, how to run it, and what to do when something goes wrong
- Prompt templates for common tasks in your business, tested on your own use cases
- An operations runbook that documents every system, workflow and integration in one place
- "How we built it" guides that explain the architecture behind a system, so your team can make informed decisions about changes

## The Academy

The [Academy](/docs/academy) is free and self-serve. Your team can work through it between sessions, and it covers the same ground as the curriculum.

<Callout type="info">
  Every resource is specific to your business. When your systems change, the documentation changes to match.
</Callout>
