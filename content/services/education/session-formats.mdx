# Session formats

Training runs on a fixed rhythm: a weekly session with your forward deployed engineer, and a review near the end of each month. The weekly session runs at a fixed day and time agreed at the kickoff, in person or by video, as your statement of work sets out.

## The weekly session

Your engineer, every week, with whoever you want in the room. Your statement of work sets its length. A session covers using the systems, correcting the Brain, telling a good result from a lucky one, and spotting the next workflow. Part of every session teaches one more part of the Platform on your own data.

The last ten minutes cover what is being built and what is next, so nothing arrives as a surprise. The same day, your engineer sends a one-page note: what moved, what is next, and at most three things we need from you, each with a person and a date.

## What a session can be

The weekly slot takes the form the week needs.

| Format | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Kickoff | Your first working session: your operations, your priorities, and who on your team learns what |
| Prompt engineering workshop | Hands-on practice with AI tools on your real business scenarios |
| Workflow workshop | How each system built for you works, how to check it, and where it could do more |
| Team training | The wider team learns to use the systems and read their results |
| Coaching | One to one, on a real problem, such as a prompt that isn't working or a result you can't explain |
| Opportunity mapping | Finding the next work worth taking off your people, across the business |
| Guided build | Your team takes part in building a system, so it can extend that system later |
| Strategy review | What is working, what to build next, and how the plan should change |

## The monthly review

Thirty minutes near the end of each month, with you and whoever else you want in the room. It covers what shipped, what it is being used for, who uses the Platform each week, and what comes next.

## Between sessions

Message your engineer between sessions and expect an answer the same business day. You can also schedule time through [Book a Call](/docs/platform/features/booking-and-scheduling).
