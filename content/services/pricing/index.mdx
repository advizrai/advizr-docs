# How pricing works

An Advizr engagement is one monthly retainer. It covers the three layers we sell as one: the Advizr Platform, the custom systems we build on top of it, and the forward deployed engineers who set the strategy, build the systems and train your team.

We do not publish prices. The proposal sets the price after the mapping calls, once we know which systems are worth building in your business.

## How the price gets set

<Steps>
  <Step title="A first call">
    We learn what slows your business down and who does that work today. There is no price on this call. If we are not the right fit, we say so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mapping calls">
    Our engineers map how your operations run: the workflows, the volumes, the handoffs and the tools your team uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The proposal">
    It names the systems we would build and puts a measured value on each one, such as hours saved a week or dollars saved a year. Then it states the monthly price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You decide">
    Take the time you need. The proposal is yours to keep whether or not you sign it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you sign, your statement of work (SOW) fixes the fee. It changes only if both sides sign an amendment.

## What the retainer covers

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| The Advizr Platform | Configured for you on day one, on infrastructure isolated to your company: its own repository, database and deployment. It holds the Brain over your documents, AI coworkers led by a Chief of Staff, workflows and integrations. |
| Custom systems | Built on the Platform around the processes that move your business. Once the first systems are live, we build the next one each month under the same fee. |
| Forward deployed engineers | A named engineer who sets the strategy with you, builds the systems, runs a weekly session with your team and answers your messages the same business day. |
| Training | Your team learns on its own data and its own systems until working with AI is how the work gets done. |

There is no build fee, no setup fee and no maintenance fee. Any tool or seat we need to build or run your systems is our cost. Your proposal and your SOW state how model usage is covered.

## The guarantee

We deliver your custom strategy and systems in under a month, or we keep working for free until they are done. [The guarantee](/docs/services/guarantees) explains it, along with the commitments your SOW makes.

## Why one retainer

A system nobody uses is worth nothing, so the engineers who build your systems stay to train the people who use them. The retainer also keeps the next system coming. Each month your team finds more work worth taking off its people, and we build it under the same fee. Models get better and cheaper every year, and on the Platform each better model is an upgrade you already have.

We price the work, not our hours. You are paying for systems that run and a team that uses them.
