# How pricing works

Advizr offers three engagement tiers. All three include both Build and Educate, which are never sold separately. What you pay scales with the scope of systems built and the depth of training delivered.

We do not publish a rate card, and this page is not a redacted one. A number here would either be wrong for your business or so wide it tells you nothing. What we can tell you is exactly how the number gets set, and when you see it.

## How the number gets set

<Steps>
  <Step title="You tell us what is broken">
    A 30-minute call. We look at the workflows, the volumes, and who does the work today. If we are not the right fit, we say so on this call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We scope it in writing">
    You get a proposal naming the systems we would build, the order we would build them in, what your team learns along the way, and the measurement we would hold ourselves to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The price is fixed before work starts">
    One number for the build, one for the monthly, both in writing, both agreed before anything begins. It does not move because the work turned out harder than we thought.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You decide">
    No pressure and no expiry games. The proposal is yours whether or not you sign it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The build fee covers discovery, design, and deployment of your first systems. The monthly covers ongoing builds, education sessions, advisory, and support.

## Why we price this way

As AI tools become easier to build, the pure build model gets commoditized. Anyone will eventually be able to wire together automations. The sustainable value is in knowing what to build, why, and how to make an organization actually adopt it.

This is why every tier includes education. The build anchors the relationship. The education drives the ongoing value. Together they deliver transformation that compounds.

It is also why we quote scope rather than hours. You are not buying our time. You are buying a system that works and a team that can run it.

## What every tier includes

- **Build and Educate combined** — systems and training delivered simultaneously
- **5x ROI guarantee** — payback within 30 days of deployment, or we work for free
- **Cancel anytime** — no long-term contracts, no lock-in periods
- **Adoption guarantee** — we make sure your team actually uses what we build
- **Dedicated engineer** — a real person assigned to your engagement
- **Documentation** — everything built is documented and explained

No hidden fees and no surprise charges. The price you agree to is the price you pay.
