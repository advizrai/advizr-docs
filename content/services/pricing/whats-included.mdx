# What's included

Advizr has no tiers. One monthly retainer covers everything on this page, for every client. Your proposal and your statement of work (SOW) set the systems, the order and the dates for your business.

## The three layers

| Layer | What you get |
|---|---|
| The Advizr Platform | Configured for you on day one, on infrastructure isolated to your company: its own repository, database and deployment. Your people sign in to one place for the Brain, Interact, Tasks, workflows, the Inbox and the Digest. |
| Custom systems | Built on the Platform around the processes that move your business. Each one reads from the Brain, starts from Tasks or Interact, and sends its results to your Inbox or Digest. Every run shows in Runs. |
| Forward deployed engineers | A named engineer who sets the strategy with you, builds the systems and trains your team. They answer your messages the same business day. |

## Training

Training is part of the retainer because systems are useless unless people use them.

| What | How it runs |
|---|---|
| The weekly session | Your engineer, every week, with whoever you want in the room. It covers using the systems, correcting the Brain, telling a good result from a lucky one, and spotting the next workflow. Part of every session teaches one more part of the Platform on your own data. |
| The monthly review | Thirty minutes on what shipped, who uses it each week, and what comes next. |
| A guide for each system | For each system you accept, a short walkthrough video and a written guide, with one part for the people who use it and one for whoever looks after it. |
| The curriculum | Four phases, from AI foundations to leading adoption, taught on your own systems. No tier holds a phase back. See [the curriculum](/docs/services/education/curriculum). |
| The Academy | Free lessons your team can take between sessions. See [the Academy](/docs/academy). |

## Each month

Each month starts with a one-page scope note: what will ship and the test each item must pass. It ends with a delivery record: each item, the test it was held to and the evidence. In between, your engineer builds, runs the weekly session and sends a one-page note after it. Once your first systems are live, the next one is built under the same fee.

## Running costs

There is no build fee, no setup fee and no maintenance fee. Hosting, monitoring and releases for your Platform are covered. Any tool or seat we need to build or run your systems is our cost. Your proposal and your SOW state how model usage is covered.

If a system creates usage inside your own software accounts, such as queries against your own data warehouse, that vendor bills you directly. We name that cost before the work starts.

## The commitments behind it

We deliver your custom strategy and systems in under a month, or we keep working for free until they are done. Your SOW states your exact commitments, and [the guarantee](/docs/services/guarantees) lists the standard ones.

<Callout type="info">
  If you stop, everything we built for you keeps running and we keep hosting it. You pay what the hosting and the model usage cost us, with nothing added, and the tools we use to build new systems switch off. Your data is yours, and we export it on request within 15 business days.
</Callout>
