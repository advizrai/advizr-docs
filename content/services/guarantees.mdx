# The delivery guarantee

Your custom strategy and systems, delivered in under a month. Or we work for free until they are.

In full: we deliver your custom strategy and systems in under a month, or we keep working for free until they are done. The clock starts on the start date in your statement of work (SOW). If we miss it, the remedy is our time, and we keep working at no charge until the work is done.

## What it is worth

Before we build, the proposal puts a measured value on each system. For example: the hours a workflow saves each week, and what those hours are worth a year. There is no blanket multiple, because a multiple says nothing about the workflow in front of you. [How we measure value](/docs/services/roi-methodology) shows the counting.

## What your SOW commits to

Your SOW states your exact commitments, and where it differs from this page, your SOW governs. These are the standard commitments in our SOW.

| Commitment | What it means |
|---|---|
| Live in 14 days | Your first system is live for your people within 14 days of the start date, or the first month is on us. Only something we need from you arriving late moves that date, and only by the days it is late. |
| Nothing accepted, nothing owed | A month in which you accept none of the agreed work is on us. Work not yet accepted carries into the next month and is finished there at no extra charge. |
| Acceptance in writing | Each piece of work is tested against criteria agreed before it starts. If it misses one, we fix it at no extra charge. |
| In use every week by day 30 | If the people you named are not using the systems every week by day 30, we add coaching and rework what is in the way, at no extra charge, until they are. |
| Months stand alone | Accepting one month does not commit you to the next. |
| A measured baseline | The numbers we report against are measured with your team on real work in the first week. Each one records who gave it, on what date, and how it was measured. |
| No return multiple | We do not promise a return on investment multiple. Each system gets its own measured value instead. |

## If you stop

You can end the engagement in any month, by email, with no penalty and no exit fee. It ends at the close of that month. Everything we built keeps running, and we keep hosting it. You pay what the hosting and the model usage cost us, with nothing added. The tools we use to build new systems switch off. If you would rather leave completely, we export everything that is yours in a standard format within 15 business days.

## Why we can offer it

We map your operations before we scope anything, so the SOW describes work we already understand. The Advizr Platform is already built and is configured for you on day one, so the first month goes into your strategy and your systems.
